SECTION B – FINANCE

PBOs stationed in Kenya should give a detailed asset inventory in accordance to Section 15(1d) of the PBO Act. PBOs operating in other countries should provide a summary of assets held in those countries.

Asset Guidelines & Notes

  1. Asset Valuation: All assets should be quoted at cost in Kenya Shillings.
  2. Land Disclosure: Land should be disclosed both in acreage and value.
  3. Biological Assets: Refer to living plants and animals.
  4. Investment Securities: Include shares, virtual assets, securities, and bonds.
  5. Asset Loss Documentation: Provide documentation on circumstances leading to the loss of assets and the action taken.
  6. Ownership Requirement: List only assets that are fully owned by the PBO.
  7. Asset Disposal Approval: PBOs must seek approval from the Authority before disposal of non-current assets (fixed assets).

Regulatory Requirement for Officials Information

Section 32 of the PBO Act requires every registered public benefit organization to provide the Authority with complete information about all members of its governing body within one month following any appointment or election. This legal requirement ensures transparency and accountability in organizational leadership and allows the Authority to maintain current records of who is responsible for organizational decisions. You must include the names, physical addresses, business addresses, and residential addresses for all three top officials listed above. These details enable the Authority to communicate directly with leadership and verify organizational governance compliance. Failure to provide this information or delays in notification may result in regulatory penalties or potential suspension of your PBO's registration status.
