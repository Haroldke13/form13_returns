PBO Week 2026 Programme Schedule
Explore the official week-long programme for PBO Week 2026, themed “A New Dawn in Action”. The week combines a high-level national launch, a three-day national exhibition, thematic forums, and customized regional or county activities designed to celebrate impact, strengthen compliance, and deepen collaboration across the sector.

Across the week, PBO Week 2026 combines national visibility, thematic discussion, exhibitions, and regional activation.
