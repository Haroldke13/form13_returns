PBO WEEK is A Flagship Annual Partnership event anchored in Celebrating, Connecting and Collaborating public benefit organizations (PBOs).

A Premier National Platform for:

  • Showcasing the critical role, impact and contribution of PBOs in Kenya's development agenda
  • Multi-stakeholder engagement, inter-sectoral connection, collaboration and partnership building
  • Knowledge and Innovation exchange

PBO WEEK 2026 is strategically designed to commemorate A NEW DAWN of renewal, reform, opportunity and collective action under the PBO Act.
